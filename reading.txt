
Reading: Final Project Submission Guidelines and Deliverables
Estimated Time: 2 minutes

You have completed series of labs applying key Unsupervised Machine Learning algorithms through hands-on project.

In this final project you will use the tools and techniques you learned throughout this course to develop a few clustering models or dimension reduction transformations on a data set that you feel passionate about. Then, you will select the algorithm that best suits your needs, and record the insights you found.

How can you submit the deliverables
You can submit your project deliverables in one of the following ways:

Option 1: AI-Graded Submission and Evaluation

Complete all required tasks of the final project as instructed.
Document and save your actions and corresponding outputs in a pdf.
Submit the file for AI-based evaluation.
Once submitted, you will receive automated grades instantly.
Option 2: Peer-Graded Submission and Evaluation

Complete all required tasks of the final project as instructed.
Document and save your actions and corresponding outputs in a pdf.
Submit the file for evaluation.
In this option, you will receive your grades based on the evaluation from other peers.
We recommend using Option 1 for faster grading. However, if you experience difficulties with this method or cannot access it, you may choose Option 2 instead.

Note: You are required to choose only one submission option either AI-Graded or Peer-Graded, and not both, to receive grades for the final assignment.
